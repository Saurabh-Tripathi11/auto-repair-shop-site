How we do things here

I opened this shop in 2004 after eleven years at a Ford dealer, and most of the people who come in now were sent by somebody we already fixed a car for. That only works if we tell people the truth about what their car needs — including when it doesn't need anything. If you've been quoted something you're not sure about, bring the estimate in and I'll look at it with you.
